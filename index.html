ViceDrop Uploader

ViceDrop Uploader is a small personal tool used by the owner of the ViceDrop YouTube channel to upload and schedule the channel's own short videos and to set their titles, descriptions and tags through the YouTube Data API.

The tool is not offered to the public. It only works with the YouTube channel of the person who authorizes it.
